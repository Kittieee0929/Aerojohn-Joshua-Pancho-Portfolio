A short hands-on exercise in transferring fictional order records, standardizing formats and checking accuracy. Built as a practice resource — not a paid client project or a professional certification.

5 records · 5 fields each · no sign-in

How to practice

  1. Read each source record carefully.
  2. Type the date as YYYY-MM-DD, copy the product name exactly, and enter numeric quantity and price.
  3. Normalize the payment status to Paid or Pending.
  4. Click Check accuracy and fix flagged entries. You can recheck as often as you like.

Tip: Always verify information against the source; don't invent missing details.
